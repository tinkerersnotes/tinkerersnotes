Projected Delaunay triangulations

Points move around a box and bounce off its walls, each with the speed and direction it got at the start. Every frame, they are projected onto the three coordinate planes, and each projection is triangulated by a divide and conquer algorithm: xOy on the floor, yOz and zOx on the back walls. The edges of each projection's convex hull also join the points in the box, in the same colour. An edge on more than one hull mixes their colours as light does: yellow, magenta or cyan for two, white for all three (light grey on a light background). Drag to turn the box.
