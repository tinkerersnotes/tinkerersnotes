Moving Delaunay triangulation

Every frame, a divide and conquer algorithm triangulates the points from scratch. It splits them by a line through their centre of mass, joins the halves' convex hulls by their two bridges, and flips edges until every triangle's circumcircle is empty. Joining the circumcircles' centres across each edge gives the dual, the Voronoi diagram; a hull edge's dual is a ray to infinity, drawn to the edge of the box. The points drift and bounce off the walls, each with the speed and direction it got at the start, or repel each other with an acceleration falling off with the square of their distance, slowed by friction until they come to rest.
