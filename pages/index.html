Demos

Seam carving

Seam carving shrinks a picture by removing its cheapest paths of pixels, keeping what stands out. Both demos run in the browser, from the same seam carver as the Python modules in the repository.

Seam carver

Shrink a picture's width and height and compare engines: the C++ core in WebAssembly, its JavaScript port, C++ on several threads and WebGPU.

Seam carving terrain

The picture as a 3D landscape, raised by the cost of reaching each pixel. The cheapest seam crosses the ridge at its lowest pass. Carve and watch the terrain change.

Triangulation

Delaunay triangulation connects points into triangles whose circumcircles hold no other point. The demo runs a JavaScript port of the repository's divide and conquer triangulation.

Moving Delaunay triangulation

Up to 2000 points bounce around a box, triangulated from scratch every frame, with their convex hull. Choose how many, pause, or start them over.

Projected Delaunay triangulations

Points move in a 3D box, projected onto its three coordinate planes, each projection triangulated every frame in red, green or blue. Their hulls' edges join the points in the box too.
